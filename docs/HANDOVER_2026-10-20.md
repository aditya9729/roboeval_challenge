## 4. Interpretation (hypotheses, not facts)

1. The dominant problem is **train → dev generalization of the pi0.5 fine-tune**, not optimizer settings or step count. The model fits training demos (E013 train 0.70) and does not transfer to held-out demos of the same tasks (HPO dev ≥ 1.08).
2. Something in the **data/observation contract** may still be wrong in a way that only shows up out-of-distribution (e.g. camera/state ordering, EE-delta scaling, chunk alignment). E013's train-split result argues against a gross bug, but a subtle one is not excluded; the official-evaluator numbers are so low that a defect visible only in closed loop remains a live hypothesis.
3. Secondary: 10 Euler steps × K=1 sampling at inference is noise-dominated (E013 K=4 vs K=1); inference-time averaging or more flow steps may help once the model has signal, but will not fix a model that does not generalize.
